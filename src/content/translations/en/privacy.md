**Shardee Privacy Policy**

This Privacy Policy applies to the use of services provided by the mobile application **Shardee** (the "App" or the "Company"), offered by **DIARO** (the "Operator").

Last updated: October 14, 2026

<h2 id="section-1">1. Purpose of this Privacy Policy</h2>

This Privacy Policy explains how Shardee collects, uses, stores and protects user information. It complies with applicable laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA).

Shardee is designed around the principle of collecting as little personal information as possible. Most user-generated content is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to Company servers.

This Policy may be revised from time to time due to changes in privacy laws, related guidelines or internal product policies. Revisions will be announced through this Policy, which users may check at any time.

<h2 id="section-2">2. Information we collect</h2>

Shardee collects only the minimum information needed to provide and improve its services.

### A. Information needed to provide App features

Shardee may use the following device permissions to provide the App's core features.

- Camera access: Required to record videos.
- Microphone access: Required to record audio along with videos.
- Photo library access: Required to save recorded files to the user's device and import them when needed.
- Location access: Used, if permitted by the user, to add location metadata to recorded video files.
- Notification access: Used, if permitted by the user, to send recording reminders and other notifications related to App use.
- Health access (optional): Used to display step counts in videos.

Videos and audio recorded by users are stored locally on their devices by default. They are not uploaded to or stored on Company servers.

Recording-location coordinates, addresses and place names are not transmitted to Shardee servers, Firebase Analytics or PostHog. However, if a user shares a file containing location metadata with another app or person, the location information in that file may also be shared. The processing of approximate location inferred from IP addresses is described in the usage analytics and error diagnostics section below.

### B. Usage analytics and error diagnostics data

Shardee uses Google Firebase Analytics and PostHog to improve service quality and analyze feature usage. On Android, Firebase Analytics processes usage events, purchase events, app instance and advertising identifiers, and approximate location inferred from IP addresses. Sentry processes crash, unresponsiveness and error diagnostics, App, device and operating system information, installation identifiers and IP addresses. Automatic error diagnostics operate separately from usage analytics consent settings. Original videos and photos, screenshots, the body of edited text, and health values read from health services are not sent as diagnostic information.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), usage analytics data, including Firebase data, is collected after consent is given on the consent screen shown before the first onboarding survey. On Android, usage analytics is enabled by default outside the EEA.

Information that may be collected:

- App usage patterns
- Frequency of feature use
- Screen navigation information
- Error logs and performance data
- Device model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Approximate location inferred from IP addresses (country, region and city)
- App instance, installation and device identifiers

This information is used for the following purposes.

- Improving App stability
- Analyzing feature usage
- Improving the user experience

This information does not directly identify individuals. Analytics events do not include original videos or photos recorded or edited by users, thumbnails, the body of edited text, precise location coordinates, addresses, place names, step-count values read from health services or increases in those values.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) can withdraw or change their consent at any time in App Settings > Privacy and Terms > Usage Analytics.

### C. Information collected when using the contact feature

When users use the App's contact feature, the inquiries they provide may be processed to receive ideas, investigate complaints, contact users, send notices and communicate the outcome of handling an inquiry.

- Email address or Instagram/Threads account information (optional)
- Inquiry content
- Attached images (optional)
- Device model information
- Operating system version
- App version information

Inquiries may be processed through external collaboration tools such as Discord to help the team provide customer support.

The collected information is used only to respond to customer inquiries.

Please do not include health information, such as step counts, or sensitive personal information in inquiries or attached images.

The Company does not use this information for purposes other than responding to inquiries.

### D. In-app purchase information

Premium features are provided through the Apple App Store in-app purchase system on iOS and Google Play on Android. The Android App uses purchase history and purchase tokens to verify and restore purchase status. When usage analytics is enabled, it processes product, amount, currency and transaction identifiers as purchase events in Firebase Analytics.

Payment information is processed by Apple or Google. Shardee does not collect or store payment card information or billing information.

Purchase history is linked to the user's Apple account. Purchases can be restored after reinstalling the App when signed in with the same Apple account.

Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

### E. Apple Health data

If a user chooses the step-count feature and grants permission, the App reads only step counts from Apple Health to display walking activity in videos. It does not add or change health data.

Values read are processed only in memory on the device. They are not sent to servers or analytics services, or used for advertising or marketing.

The selected step count is included in the exported video.

Permission can be withdrawn in Apple Health. Videos that have already been saved will not change.

### F. Android Health Connect data

Displaying step counts in Shardee is optional. After a user selects the feature, agrees to the notice and grants Android permission, the App reads total step counts from Health Connect for the dates and periods when the photos and videos included in an edit were recorded (READ_STEPS). This is used to display walking activity for a day or for the video's period in the preview and in the video exported by the user.

When editing media recorded more than 30 days ago, the App uses access to historical health data (READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY) to read step counts for that period. It does not read health data other than step counts or add or modify Health Connect records.

The values read are processed on the device and are not saved as health records in editing projects or presets. During export, temporary images or videos containing step counts may be created in the App's on-device workspace. Exported videos include the selected step counts and remain on the device until the user deletes them. Uninstalling the App or withdrawing permission alone does not delete videos in the photo library or copies shared with other people.

Health values read are not transmitted to Company servers, advertising services or analytics services, and are not used for sale, advertising or marketing. If a user shares a video containing step counts with another app or person, the step counts visible in that video are also shared. Users can check the contents in the preview before sharing and remove the step-count display if they do not want it included.

Access permission can be withdrawn at any time in Android Health Connect. Normal recording and editing features remain available without connecting Health Connect or when permission is denied. Original health records can be managed and deleted in Health Connect. Exported videos can be deleted from the photo library or from the app holding a copy.

The Company retains personal information only for as long as necessary.
